The duo

About us

You'd swear a full band was hiding in the wings.

Deni's virtuosic electric violin soars over Chris's driving guitar, and the two trade lead vocals and interlocking harmonies. Her credits run from NPR's Mountain Stage to Cyndi Lauper, R.E.M. and Sarah McLachlan. Having graced stages from living rooms to Carnegie Hall, the duo delivers a warm, spirited show that leaves every audience smiling, energized, and completely connected.
